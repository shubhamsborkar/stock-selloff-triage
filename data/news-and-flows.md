## News timeline

### Tue Jun 3 — Broadcom fiscal Q2 FY26 earnings (after close)
- Revenue $22.19B vs $22.13B consensus; non-GAAP EPS $2.44 vs $2.39 expected — a beat on both lines. Profit, cash flow, and AI sales hit records.
  - Source: [CNBC earnings report](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/03/broadcom-avgo-earnings-report-q2-2026.html), [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/broadcom-nasdaq-avgo-stock-plunges-14-due-to-weaker-than-expected-revenue), [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/06/04/broadcom-sinks-14-on-soft-ai-chip-outlook-despite-earnings-beat-dragging-down-amd-and-intel/)
- The problem was guidance: fiscal Q3 AI chip sales guided to ~$16B vs ~$17.2B analyst expectation, and CEO Hock Tan did NOT raise the full-year ~$100B AI semiconductor sales target.
  - Source: [Motley Fool Jun 4 market wrap](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/06/04/stock-market-today-june-4-broadcom-shares-plunge-after-ai-outlook-misses-high-investor-expectations/), [Motley Fool warning-flag piece](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/06/04/the-broadcom-sell-off-why-this-is-a-huge-warning-f/)

### Wed Jun 4 — AVGO breaks, drags the AI complex
- AVGO closed −12.6% at $418.91, roughly $280B of market value erased.
  - Source: [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8900763/broadcom-avgo-downgraded-to-neutral-following-q2-earnings-report), [24/7 Wall St](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/06/04/broadcom-sinks-14-on-soft-ai-chip-outlook-despite-earnings-beat-dragging-down-amd-and-intel/)
- Macquarie downgraded AVGO Outperform → Neutral, price target $513 → $437, citing risk that Google insources more of its custom ASIC work — a direct hit to the custom-AI-chip thesis.
  - Source: [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8900763/broadcom-avgo-downgraded-to-neutral-following-q2-earnings-report)
- Counterpoint: Bank of America RAISED its price target to $530 (from $450) on CY27 earnings.
  - Source: [TheStreet via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/bank-america-resets-broadcom-stock-033300479.html)
- Micron, Marvell, Intel and the broader chip complex sold off in sympathy.
  - Source: [CNBC chip stocks](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/04/chipmaker-equities-micron-marvell-broadcom-intel.html), [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/markets/broadcoms-underwhelming-results-are-dragging-the-ai-complex-and-the-wider-market-lower/)
- Same-day press already framed it as "rotation into other sectors"; Iran war concerns also in the mix (energy prices).
  - Source: [TheStreet Jun 4 market blog](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-june-4-2026-nasdaq-futures-slide-amid-iran-war-concerns)

### Fri Jun 5 — Hot jobs report turns a chip problem into a market problem
- May nonfarm payrolls +172k vs +80k consensus; unemployment steady at 4.3%. "Good news" read as inflationary: CME FedWatch odds of a Fed rate HIKE by December jumped to 70% (from ~45% a week earlier).
  - Source: [Motley Fool Jun 5](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/06/05/stock-market-today-june-5-strong-jobs-data-drives-broad-sell-off-at-midday/), [Fortune/Barclays](https://fortune.com/2026/06/07/nasdaq-selloff-ai-bubble-rally-jobs-report-inflation/)
- 10-year Treasury yield surged above 4.53%.
  - Source: [CNBC yields](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/treasury-yields-ease-as-traders-await-key-labor-market-data-.html)
- Nasdaq −4.2%, worst day since April 2025; S&P 500 −2.6%. Philadelphia Semiconductor Index −10.3%, steepest one-day fall since 2020; >$1T (some tallies $1.3T globally) wiped from chip-sector market cap.
  - Source: [CNBC live updates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/04/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [TheStreet Jun 5](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-june-05-2026), [Intellectia](https://intellectia.ai/blog/semiconductor-stocks-selloff-june-2026)
- Single names that day: SOXX −10.4%; ARM −12.8%, Intel −11.3%, AMD −10.9%. Leveraged SOXL −30.5%.
  - Source: [StartupHub.ai daily](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-stocks-daily/2026/ai-stocks-2026-06-05), [eciks/SOXL](https://eciks.org/7659-38039-soxl-stock-rebounds-broadcom-earnings)
- Contagion to Asia: Kospi −5.54% (Samsung −6.4%, SK Hynix −9.9%).
  - Source: [CNBC live updates](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/04/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)
- Background themes cited alongside AVGO: memory-chip pricing crisis and weak projected smartphone demand.
  - Source: [Kavout](https://www.kavout.com/market-lens/what-triggered-the-recent-semiconductor-sell-off), [Intellectia](https://intellectia.ai/blog/ai-semiconductor-selloff-june-2026)
